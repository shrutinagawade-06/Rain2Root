HOW IT WORKS

From Rooftop to Groundwater

A simple process for collecting rain and directing suitable water toward storage or groundwater recharge.

🌧️

01. Collection

Roof surfaces and gutters collect rainfall and guide it to a downpipe.

🪨

02. Filtration

A suitable filter removes leaves and suspended particles before storage or recharge.

💧

03. Storage

Collected water can be stored in a tank for appropriate non-drinking uses.

🌱

04. Recharge

Suitable excess water can be directed to a properly designed recharge structure.
